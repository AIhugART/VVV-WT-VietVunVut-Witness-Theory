## 5. RCA Consolidation Findings

### Finding 1 — BIAN-4 is now the largest overlap cluster

Root cause: representation is not a single source-side concept. It includes representation theory, object-as-appearance, external-object status, resemblance/form-bearing, and representative perception.

RCA decision: keep all five draft bridges separate until a final bridge-uniqueness review decides whether to merge, rank, or retain them as distinct stable IDs.

### Finding 2 — BIAN-16 has multi-level source support

Root cause: measurement self-completion can be supported at result level, formula/decomposition level, reciprocal dependence level, and means/result non-distinction level.

RCA decision: keep all five BIAN-16 draft bridges as support evidence. Do not infer a new VVV-QMRF postulate unless a separate RCA proves a new registration-layer function not covered by E1-E16.

### Finding 3 — Negative evidence splits across BIAN-14, BIAN-15, and BIAN-18

Root cause: negative evidence can function as validity condition, contrastive condition, or null/absence-side condition depending on context.

RCA decision: preserve boundaries among negative concomitance, contrastive evidence, and nonoccurrence. Do not collapse them into a single final bridge yet.
