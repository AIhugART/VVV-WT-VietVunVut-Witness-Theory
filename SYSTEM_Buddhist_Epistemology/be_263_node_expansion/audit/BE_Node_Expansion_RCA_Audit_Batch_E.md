## 5. RCA 5 Whys Summary

### Why only 5 of 50 Batch E nodes are marked as candidates?

1. Why not promote all Batch E nodes? Because Batch E is dominated by text nodes (2), word-category nodes (5), trairupya sub-components (6), duplicate concepts (~15), historical/method nodes (3), and soteriology (2).
2. Why does duplication matter? Because the 30 core nodes already cover perception, inference, trairupya, apoha, arthakriya, svalaksana, samanyalaksana, and pararthanumana.
3. Why are the 5 candidates distinct enough? Yojana isolates superimposition process; Dharmakirti's anti-realism gives ontology-boundary language; four process mechanisms give a distinct registration-sequence model; Darsana isolates sign-observation; partial property focus isolates encoding selectivity.
4. Why keep other nodes as evidence-only rather than no-map? Because even sub-components and duplicates can serve as source-evidence anchors for existing BIANs without creating new bridge rows.
5. Root cause: Batch E has high source richness for logic and language but is structurally dense with duplicates; strict triage prevents bridge-bloat while preserving evidence for existing BIAN supports.

RCA decision: keep 45 nodes outside promotion and send 5 nodes to a later promotion-gate mini-audit.
