## Result

**No prior work identifies the Superobserver's polar angle θ as a relevant or
tunable parameter in EWF experiments.** Azimuthal angles are extensively
optimized and reported. θ is implicitly fixed to π/2 without comment, treated
as a default configuration rather than a degree of freedom.
