## S3.1 — Relation to Contextuality

## S3.2 — Physical Picture: Measurement Incompatibility Between Observers

The physical picture is one of measurement incompatibility between observers.
When a Friend measures in the z-basis, they produce a macroscopic record with
a definite orientation on the Bloch sphere. A Superobserver measuring at Bloch
angles (θ, φ) probes this record at a relative angle determined by the basis
overlap |⟨b|d⟩|².

Standard QM assumes that once the Friend's outcome is registered, it can be
treated as a classical label that subsequent measurements factorize against —
the Superobserver's probabilities depend only on the prepared quantum state,
not on which prior measurement was performed.

Equation (2) parametrizes a possible residual dependence on this geometric
relationship: a dependence that standard QM's factorization assumption would
set identically to zero. Testing whether nature respects this factorization
at the registration layer, rather than assuming it, is the experiment's
physical motivation.

## S3.3 — Relation to Quantum Interpretations

The experiment is interpretation-neutral by design. None predicts Eq. (2-3);
none precludes it. We briefly note how each major interpretation relates.

**Many-Worlds (MWI).** MWI treats all branches symmetrically — no mechanism
for overlap-dependent deviation — but does not exclude geometric structure
in cross-branch registration. A positive result would challenge the assumption
that branch weights are determined solely by the Born rule.

**Relational Quantum Mechanics (RQM).** Eq. (2) parametrizes the "cost" of
relating outcomes across incompatible observer perspectives: |⟨b|d⟩|²
measures observer-perspective compatibility. RQM is naturally compatible with
cross-observer geometric structure but predicts no specific β.

**Copenhagen.** The Friend's outcome is a classical fact; the Superobserver's
measurement is independent. Copenhagen does not predict β ≠ 0, but the
factorization assumption (Superobserver statistics independent of prior
measurement) is an additional postulate beyond the core axioms.

**QBism.** Eq. (2) parametrizes a possible systematic deviation from
Born-rule belief updating when measurement bases are misaligned. QBism is
agnostic — the experiment provides empirical calibration of the update rule.

**Objective Collapse.** Collapse models modify state evolution; Eq. (2)
modifies probability assignment at measurement. The two are distinguishable:
collapse depends on system mass/size, Eq. (2) on basis geometry. A θ-sweep
could separate the signatures.
